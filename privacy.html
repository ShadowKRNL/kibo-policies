Privacy Policy

How KIBO collects, uses, and handles information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how KIBO, a Discord bot developed by Kernel, handles information when users and Discord servers interact with the Bot.

By using KIBO, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

KIBO may collect and store information necessary to provide its order and ticket management features, including:

  • Discord User IDs associated with customers and sellers.
  • Product names and order details.
  • Product quantities and remaining stock.
  • Product image links.
  • Order statuses, such as completed or pending.
  • Information required to manage and track orders.

3. Order Information

Order-related information is stored in KIBO's database to support order management, inventory tracking, and order status updates.

This information is used only for the functionality and operation of the Bot.

4. Tickets and Transcripts

KIBO can generate HTML transcripts of ticket conversations.

These transcripts are sent as files to designated Discord channels within the server. They are not stored in KIBO's order database.

Transcripts may contain messages, Discord usernames, user IDs, and attachments shared during ticket conversations.

Access to these transcripts depends on the permissions and configuration of the Discord server.

5. Payments and Financial Information

KIBO does not directly process payments or collect payment card details, banking credentials, or financial account information.

Any payment arrangements made between users are handled outside the Bot.

6. How Information Is Used

Collected information may be used to:

  • Create and manage order records.
  • Track product quantities and availability.
  • Identify customers and sellers through Discord User IDs.
  • Generate ticket transcripts.
  • Maintain and improve KIBO's functionality.
  • Provide support and resolve order-related issues.

7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

KIBO does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed or stored through Discord and the hosting or infrastructure providers required to operate the Bot.

Ticket transcripts are sent to Discord channels configured by server administrators.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Order information may remain in the database for as long as it is needed to provide KIBO's services. The exact retention period depends on the Bot's data management practices.

Ticket transcripts remain subject to Discord's storage and server management settings. Server administrators may manage transcript messages according to their permissions.

For questions or requests concerning stored order information, contact Kernel through its official Discord community.

9. Data Security

Kernel takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by KIBO. However, no online service or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

KIBO is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or Discord's age requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KIBO's features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
