Privacy Policy

How KIBO handles information across its ticket and support management services.

1. Introduction

KIBO is a Discord bot developed by Kernel, primarily designed to help Discord communities manage support tickets, organize support teams, and handle customer requests.

This Privacy Policy explains what information KIBO handles, how it is used, and where certain information is stored.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features used, KIBO may handle the following information:

  • Discord User IDs associated with customers and support staff.
  • Ticket information and related support activity.
  • Messages and attachments included in ticket conversations and transcripts.
  • Order details, product names, quantities, and remaining stock.
  • Product image links and order statuses.
  • Seller and customer Discord IDs associated with orders.

3. Ticket Management

KIBO uses information related to support tickets to provide its ticket management functionality.

This may include identifying ticket participants, organizing support requests, and helping support teams manage customer conversations.

4. Ticket Transcripts and Storage

KIBO can generate HTML transcripts of ticket conversations.

Generated transcripts are sent as files to designated Discord channels within the relevant server.

Ticket transcripts are not stored in KIBO's order database. They are stored as messages or attachments within Discord.

Transcripts may contain messages, usernames, Discord User IDs, and attachments shared during ticket conversations.

Access to transcripts depends on Discord server permissions and channel configuration.

5. Order Information

KIBO may store order-related information in its database to support order management and tracking.

This information may include:

  • Product names and descriptions.
  • Product quantities and remaining stock.
  • Product image links.
  • Seller and customer Discord User IDs.
  • Order statuses, including completed orders.

6. Payments and Financial Information

KIBO does not directly process payments or collect payment card details, banking credentials, or financial account information.

Payment arrangements between customers and sellers are handled outside KIBO.

7. How Information Is Used

Information handled by KIBO may be used to:

  • Create and manage support tickets.
  • Help support teams organize customer requests.
  • Identify ticket participants.
  • Generate and deliver ticket transcripts.
  • Manage and track order records.
  • Maintain and operate the Bot's features.

8. Data Sharing and Third Parties

KIBO does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed or stored through Discord and infrastructure providers necessary to operate the Bot.

Ticket transcripts are delivered to Discord channels configured by server administrators.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Order information is stored in KIBO's database. The applicable retention period depends on the Bot's data management practices.

Ticket transcripts are stored in Discord channels rather than KIBO's order database. Their availability depends on Discord and the server's management settings.

For questions or requests regarding order information, contact Kernel through its official Discord community.

10. Data Security

Kernel takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by KIBO. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KIBO's features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
