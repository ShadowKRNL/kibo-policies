Privacy Policy

How KIBO handles information when providing Discord ticket and support services.

1. Introduction

KIBO is a Discord bot developed by Kernel to help Discord communities manage support tickets, organize support teams, and handle customer support requests.

This Privacy Policy explains what information KIBO handles, how it is used, and where ticket information is stored.

2. Information We Handle

To provide its functionality, KIBO may handle the following information:

  • Discord User IDs of users interacting with the Bot.
  • Discord User IDs of support team members.
  • Ticket-related information.
  • Messages included in ticket conversations and transcripts.
  • Attachments shared within support tickets.
  • Information required to manage ticket statuses and workflows.

3. How Information Is Used

Information handled by KIBO may be used to:

  • Create and manage support tickets.
  • Identify ticket participants.
  • Organize support requests.
  • Help support staff manage customer inquiries.
  • Generate ticket transcripts.
  • Maintain and operate the Bot's features.

4. Ticket Transcripts and Storage

KIBO can generate HTML transcripts of ticket conversations.

Generated transcripts are sent as files to designated Discord channels within the relevant server.

Ticket transcripts are not stored in KIBO's application database. They are stored as messages or attachments within Discord.

Transcripts may contain messages, usernames, Discord User IDs, and attachments shared during ticket conversations.

Access to transcripts depends on Discord server permissions and channel configuration.

5. Data Sharing

KIBO does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed through Discord and infrastructure providers necessary to operate the Bot.

Ticket transcripts are delivered to Discord channels configured by server administrators.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information handled by KIBO may be retained for as long as necessary to provide its services.

Ticket transcripts remain within Discord channels until they are removed through the applicable Discord server management controls or other applicable retention mechanisms.

For questions or requests concerning information handled by KIBO, contact the Kernel team through its official Discord community.

7. Data Security

Kernel takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by KIBO. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Services

KIBO operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Discord Privacy Policy: View Discord Privacy Policy.
