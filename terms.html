2. About KIBO

KIBO is a Discord bot developed by Kernel, primarily designed for ticket management and customer support team operations.

The Bot helps Discord communities organize support requests, manage ticket workflows, assist support staff, and maintain organized communication between customers and server teams.

KIBO also provides additional features for order management and tracking.

3. Ticket and Support Management

KIBO provides tools that may include:

  • Creating and managing support tickets.
  • Organizing customer support requests.
  • Helping support teams handle and respond to tickets.
  • Managing ticket statuses and workflows.
  • Generating HTML transcripts of ticket conversations.
  • Sending ticket transcripts to designated Discord channels.
